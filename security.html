What this covers

This page explains in plain terms how we look after what you tell us, and what to watch out for. It sits alongside the privacy notice, which covers your legal rights over that information.

Your enquiry details

  • Enquiries reach a mailbox only Alvari Group staff can open.
  • Survey photographs and notes are kept on company devices, passcode-locked, not on personal phones.
  • Access is limited to the people who need it to quote, do the job or invoice for it.
  • We do not sell your details and we do not pass them to lead-generation companies.

Confirm each of these is actually true before publishing. If enquiries currently land on a personal phone, either change that or change this page.

This website

  • Served over HTTPS, so what you type is encrypted in transit.
  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no analytics cookies.
  • The enquiry form asks only for a name, phone, email and postcode.
  • Hosting and certificate provider: to be confirmed.

Payments

We never take card details through this website, by email, or in a phone message. There is no payment page here. Payment is arranged with you on completion, by the method set out in your quote.

If anyone asks you to pay Alvari Group by card over the phone, in cryptocurrency, or into an account you have not seen on one of our invoices — stop, and call us on 07483 172469.

How we will and will not contact you

Roofing has a known doorstep-fraud problem, so it is worth being blunt.

We will

  • reply to an enquiry you actually sent us;
  • turn up at a time you agreed, and show you who we are;
  • give you a written, itemised quote before any work starts.

We will not

  • knock on your door uninvited to say we have spotted something wrong with your roof;
  • ask for cash up front, or press you to decide on the spot;
  • ask for your bank details by email or text;
  • change our bank details by email. If you get a message saying we have, do not pay it — ring the number above first. That scam is common in this trade.

Reporting a problem

If you think someone is using our name, or you have found a security problem with this website, tell us at Alvari Group, 07483 172469, hello@alvarigroup.co.uk and we will look into it.

Suspected fraud can also be reported to Action Fraud on 0300 123 2040 or at actionfraud.police.uk.
