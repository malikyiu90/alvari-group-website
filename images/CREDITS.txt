Sizing: each file is downloaded at roughly twice its display size, so it is
sharp on a retina screen without being needlessly heavy. The hero and the
closing CTA band run full-bleed, so those two are true 4K.

Before launch, convert these to WebP or AVIF. The hero alone is about 1.3MB
as a JPEG, which is slow on mobile data; WebP typically cuts that by 60-70%
with no visible difference.

Do NOT replace these with images from a Google Images search - those are
almost always someone else's copyright and this is a commercial site.
Alvari's own job photographs are the real upgrade here, especially in the
Recent Work gallery.
