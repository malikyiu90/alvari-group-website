Why, and our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR we have to tell you which basis we rely on:

  • Steps before a contract — replying to your enquiry, surveying, quoting.
  • Performing a contract — doing the work you booked and invoicing for it.
  • Legal obligation — records HMRC requires, and any Building Control notification a job needs.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping a record of jobs so a guarantee claim can be checked years later, and defending a dispute. We have weighed that against your privacy and kept only what it needs.

We do not send marketing unless you ask for it, and you can stop it at any time.

When a period ends we delete the record or strip anything that identifies you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data and we do not pass it to lead-generation or marketing lists. We share it only with:

  • our accountant and our insurer, where a job or claim requires it;
  • a scaffolding firm or specialist subcontractor — limited to the address and access details they need;
  • your insurer or loss adjuster on storm work, with your agreement;
  • Building Control, where the work has to be notified;
  • a regulator or a court, where the law requires it.

Records are held in the UK or the EEA. If that changes we will say so here and put the required safeguards in place first.

Cookies and the map

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. There is no tracking pixel and no third-party advertising on it.

The coverage map is embedded from OpenStreetMap. When it loads, your browser contacts their servers directly and they see your IP address, as they would on any site. We receive nothing from it. Fonts load from Google Fonts the same way.

If you later add Google Analytics, a Meta pixel, a chat widget or call tracking, this section stops being true and you will need a cookie consent banner under PECR. Update this page the same day you add the tag, not afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of what we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it, where we do not need it for a contract, a guarantee or the law;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • send it to you, or to someone else, in a portable format.

Write to Alvari Group, 07483 172469, hello@alvarigroup.co.uk. We answer within one month and we do not charge for it.
