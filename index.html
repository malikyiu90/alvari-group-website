About Us

The Alvari way — “fix the cause, not the symptom”

We have been on roofs across Leicester and Leicestershire since 2008. A leak gets traced back to what is actually causing it — a failed valley, porous mortar, a slipped slate — before anyone quotes you for a roof you may not need.

Every job is surveyed properly, priced in writing and notified to Building Control where the work needs it. No deposits, no day-rate surprises, and no subcontracted strangers on your scaffold.

Our Roofing Process

A simple process from survey to sign-off

01

Call or send photos

Ring us or send a picture of the problem. We will say straight away whether it needs a survey at all.

02

Free roof survey

Ladder or drone inspection, moisture readings where needed, and photographs of every defect we find.

03

Fixed written quote

Itemised and valid for 60 days. We walk you through it and say plainly what can wait and what cannot.

04

The work itself

Scaffold up, job done with the materials on your quote, site swept at the end of every day.

05

Signed off and guaranteed

Final inspection together, waste taken away, and your written guarantee issued within five days.
